Starting Small

2026-02-28

As I read this article, I was asking myself, how many of us adults feel so when we are trying to learn a new skill?

Of course, as an adult with responsibilities, your first thing which shoots down the idea would be 'How fast can I learn this skill so as to make a living out of it?', or 'Why spend time on something which will not help you earn an income'. And everyone has commitment these days right? Car loans, credit card loans, house loans. None of this helps in letting you consider learning a new skill.

And after reading that article, I straightaway realise that how much of this barrier exists as well for people looking at Christianity. Has Christianity been a gated barrier? Well maybe not really. Attending church is not scary these days, especially the Protestant churches. Its almost like going for a concert, and if you go to a big enough church, you could practically blend in into the crowd without anyone knowing you actually, and just going with the motions.

However, regarding the Catholic church I would think this is really very much a gated community. Going into a Catholic service without a Catholic friend can be daunting. There are some hand symbols that they do which you have no idea. And if the priest isn't speaking in a clear manner, you will genuinely be lost. Blessed are you who persevere however, and blessed are you if you able to join a class such as the RCIA (Rite of Catholic Initiation for Adults).

But I think they are honest seekers out there who also look at the bible and find that it is a gateway of its own. I guess the easiest route of entry is into the Gospels. Genesis and Exodus are historical documents and read as such. In the old testament, do you find people of the other nations seeking the faith of Abraham? In the new, I think it is more obvious, especially among the Gentile believers.

But as the programming articles say ... they persist. Learning the bible and what it stands for is a journey where you must persist. But try to read enough for you to grasp a pattern or a theme, or a general view of a book. Perhaps start with the short ones. Jonah by itself is a pretty short book and in it you see God trying to affect the salvation for a city.

Akin to the programming journey as well. Start with basic html but continue doing it.
